Offer to Project

Every project starts as an offer in LumoPlan. Once the customer has signed, we upload the contract, accept the offer, and LumoPlan creates the project, sets it up in Notion and tells the team. Nobody creates projects or offers by hand in Notion any more.

Offer→Sign & send→Signed contract→Accept→Project→Team setup
Sales the offer's author LumoPlan happens automatically Hendrik & Rio project setup

The cycle, step by step

  1. Write the offer

    Sales
    LumoPlan → Offers → choose the customer → New offer
    • Pick the contract type (one-off or framework with prepayment annex), tick the services and set quantities, prices and discounts.
    • LumoPlan gives the offer its number (for example 2026-10-12_Offer-DE-2026-10-0012-Customer) and adds it to the Notion offers database with status In Preparation.
    • Content stays editable at every status. Changes save automatically.

  2. Sign and send

    Sales
    Offer toolbar → Details → Signatory, then Sign & Export
    • Choose the signatory (Silvan, Janis or Malte). Only the selected signatory can sign; their signature is placed into the PDF.
    • Send the PDF to the customer, then set the status to Waiting For Response and the Sent At date. Valid Until fills in 20 days later.

  3. Upload the signed contract

    Sales
    Offer toolbar → Files → Upload signed contract
    • The signed contract is the offer the customer signed. Add other contractual documents with Upload other documents.
    • Several files at once; PDF, image, Word or Excel, up to 20 MB each. Files are stored in LumoPlan with the offer.

  4. Accept the offer

    Sales
    Details → Status → Accepted

    Choosing Accepted opens the accept dialog. It shows the offer's files and lets you upload anything still missing.

    No contract, no acceptance. Accept offer stays disabled until at least one signed contract is uploaded, and LumoPlan refuses the change otherwise.

  5. LumoPlan creates the project

    LumoPlan

    Project

    • Named YYYY-MM Customer, with the address added if the name is taken
    • Status Pipeline / Paused, priority Normal
    • Type of invoicing from the contract type
    • Account manager: the offer's author
    • The offer's buildings join the project

    Notion

    • Project page made from the Customer Project template
    • Customer linked to its Notion customer page when the name matches one
    • Offer row linked to the new project

    Email

    • To Hendrik and Rio: which offer, which customer, the signed contract's file name
    • Links to the project and to the offer with its files

  6. Set up the project

    Hendrik & Rio
    Email link → LumoPlan project page
    • Assign the coordinator and the team, check the buildings, and move the project out of Pipeline / Paused when work starts.
    • The signed contract is one click away on the offer if questions come up.

Offer statuses

StatusMeaningWhat LumoPlan does
In PreparationBeing writtenKeeps the Notion row in sync
Waiting For ResponseSent to the customerSame
AcceptedCustomer signed; contract uploadedCreates the project, sets up Notion, emails Hendrik and Rio
ChangedThe offer had to be reworked after sendingOffers to create a new copy in In Preparation with the current content
RejectedCustomer declinedNothing else
CancelledWe withdrew the offerNothing else

Ground rules

  • Offers and projects start in LumoPlan. Don't create offer rows or project pages in Notion by hand: that is how duplicates appear.
  • Accept only in LumoPlan, and only once the signed contract is uploaded.
  • An accepted offer always keeps at least one contract. To replace it, upload the new file first, then delete the old one.
  • If the project page in Notion looks empty right after accepting, give it a minute: Notion fills in the template in the background.
