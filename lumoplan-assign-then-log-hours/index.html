LumoPlan · post-processing board · as of 4 November 2026

Assign people. They log what they worked.

Hours no longer stop anyone being assigned on the board. The board says who works where and when. The hours people log say how long it really took.

Live in production since 4 November 2026 (PR #65). The automated checks and deploys passed, but the browser tests haven't run and nobody has clicked through it on the real board yet.

What changed

The new workflow

  1. Assign Coordinator, on the board Right-click a stage and pick a person, paint with the brush, or use the assign dialog. If that person is already booked elsewhere that day, the booking still goes through.
  2. Work Everyone reads the board The board shows who is on which building and stage on which days, with no hour numbers. Stages shorter than a day are still drawn at their real length.
  3. Log The person, on the Tasks page Press Record hours on the task and enter what they really worked. Logged hours are stored separately from the plan.

Why: Rio said on 28 October that the board is planning data that will never be exact, and asked for hours to leave the timeline. Milton set the wider rule on 4 November: hours issues shouldn't block people. This replaces the settings switch and trial period in the October proposal.

What stays as it was

  • Holidays and non-working days. Those days are skipped, because the person really isn't there. Moving a stage onto one still asks: Cancel move, Move & drop or Move & keep all hours.
  • Hours behind each booking. Bookings still save a planned number, so the Tasks page and team views don't go blank. Expect people's load to read above 100% more often in those views. That is information, not a block.
  • Shortening a stage. It still asks Trim hours & shorten, Keep scheduled hours or Cancel resize when bookings would be cut.
  • Hour numbers elsewhere. They remain in the assign dialog, the brush sizes and cursor badge, a person's time distribution, the "h free" hint in the stage menu, and absence badges in the board header.

How to test

Setup. Use the dev site, because these steps create real bookings. Open plan.lumodev.cloud/postprocessing and set the top-bar toggle to Planning. Assign one person to a stage for full days, so those days are full. You need a second and third stage on the same days, and a day when that person is on holiday.

#Do thisYou should see
1Look at a stage with people on it. Hover the stage, then a person block.No hour numbers on the bar, the blocks or the hover text. Blocks show names.
2Right-click the second stage and pick the person who is already full.No prompt. A message says "Assigned … to …". Their name is on both stages on the same days.
3Press B, pick the same person, and drag across a full day on the third stage.It paints. The badge shows hours to add, such as +8h, and no dialog appears.
4With the brush, hover and then drag over that person's holiday.The badge turns red and says absent. That day isn't painted, and the message says "skipped 1 absent".
5In daily or hourly view, click an hour cell. In Assign {stage} Stage, pick the full person and enter 8 hours.It saves, with no "only has … available" warning.
6Drag a stage onto days when its person is full. Then drag it onto their holiday.The first move goes through with no dialog. The second opens a dialog with Cancel move, Move & drop and Move & keep all hours.
7Open the ⋮ menu (More actions) in the top bar.Cleanup Overbooking is gone. The other items are still there.
8Open Tasks as that person, press Record hours on today's task, and enter the real hours.The hours save and the board stays as it was. See the Personio limit under What's missing if it refuses.
