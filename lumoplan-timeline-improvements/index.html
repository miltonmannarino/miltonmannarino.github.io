MBuilding milestones

A milestone is one named date on a building's timeline with an assignee and a done tick. It is not a stage: no start and end, no hours, no place in the chain. The building's due date stays its own field. First use is Alexandra's early QC pre-check on large buildings; a later candidate is "AirThings model expected".

Rathaus Mitte
DUE 14 Nov
✓ Scans checked
⚑ QC pre-check
⚑ AirThings model

DUE keeps the top edge, milestones take the bottom. Green is done, blue is open, red is past its date and still open. Clicking a pill toggles done for any user, the same rule as marking a stage done.

PPacking Sports as an assignee

Rio and Carlos asked for the external annotation team to be assignable on the timeline even though it is not a person: a building or a preprocessing stage goes to "Packing Sports". Version one is assignment only. No mail to them, no tracking of their time.

Why it needed code

  • The board's people list is employees filtered by isTaskTeamMember: active, Customers department, lumoview mailbox.
  • The server rejects assignments for ids that are not in the employees collection.
  • Zero capacity toasts "no available hours" and the fully-booked prompt would fire on every second building.

Chosen shape (commit dc1379a)

  • One real employees record with a new isGroup flag. The Personio sync upserts by Personio id, so a hand-made id survives.
  • Kept out of the Customers department: ten pickers across the app filter on that department alone and would list the team.
  • Only the board admits groups, through isAssignableTeamMember. isTaskTeamMember and pickableEmployees exclude them.

Behaviour of a group assignee

  • Preprocessing stages only. It is not offered on postprocessing stages in the stage menu, the assignment modal, the package bulk modal or the follow-up cycle pickers. If anything still tries, the board refuses with "Packing Sports can only be assigned to preprocessing stages".
  • 24 hours a day of capacity, 0 hours allocated, so it is never "fully booked".
  • Booked by range fill only: every day of the stage, no hour schedule, no fully-booked prompt.
  • No assignment mail (reason: 'group-assignee'). Not on the team swimlane, coverage or capacity views.
  • The assignment picker shows it as "Packing Sports (team)".

Sharing stays separate (on hold)

testHow to test

All of this runs locally against the development database. Never use pnpm dev:prod for these checks.

cd ~/Documents/lumoview/lumoplan/lumoplan
pnpm dev            # http://localhost:3000, dev database

1 · Milestones

  1. Open /postprocessing and switch to planning mode.
  2. On a building row, click the calendar button to open Edit Timeline. Scroll to Milestones.
  3. Type "QC pre-check", pick a date two days ahead and yourself as assignee, click Add. The row appears in the list straight away.
  4. Close the modal. A blue flag pill "QC pre-check" sits at the bottom edge of the row on that day; the DUE pill stays at the top.
  5. Click the pill. It turns green with a tick. Click again to reopen it.
  6. Back in the modal, move the date to last week. The pill turns red (overdue).
  7. Open /my-work with the Person filter on yourself. A Milestones card under Upcoming events lists it under Overdue, and the header shows "1 milestone overdue". Tick it there; with "Open only" on it disappears.
  8. Rename it, change the assignee to Unassigned, then delete it. Each change sticks after a page reload.
  9. Turn on paint mode. Pills are still visible but no longer clickable.

2 · Packing Sports group

  1. The record already exists on dev. Check it with: 
    node scripts/packing-sports-group.mjs status        # record + its bookings, read-only
  2. Reload /postprocessing, planning mode. Right-click a preprocessing stage. "Packing Sports" is in the Assign list.
  3. Assign it. It is booked on every day of the stage, with no "fully booked" prompt and no "no available hours" toast.
  4. Assign it to a second preprocessing stage that overlaps the first. Still no fully-booked prompt.
  5. Right-click a postprocessing stage (3D postprocessing, QC, delivery). Packing Sports is not in the Assign list. Same in the assignment modal, the package bulk modal and a follow-up cycle's stage pickers; its preprocessing picker does list it.
  6. Open the assignment modal from a preprocessing stage. The picker lists "Packing Sports (team)".
  7. Check where it must not show up: the team view in My Work, the coverage view, the capacity strip, the measurement planning people pickers, the coordinator picker in the project editor.
  8. Open the assignee picker on a building row label. Packing Sports is not listed. That picker offers people only; making the group a building's assignee is still missing (see below).
  9. Check the building is not shared with Packing Sports because of the assignment (the access pill on the row stays as it was).
  10. Delete your test bookings from the board when you are done. Keep the record on dev.

3 · Board split (regressions)

Nothing should look or behave differently. In planning mode on /postprocessing:

  1. Collapse and expand a project header, change its coordinator, archive and reopen it.
  2. Right-click a stage: mark done, insert stage after, branch parallel stage, delete stage, assign and unassign a person.
  3. Select two buildings, then assign a person on one of them. The "This building only / Distribute across" prompt appears. Same for unassign.
  4. With two buildings selected, resize a stage on one. The "Apply stage length change?" prompt appears.
  5. Assign someone who is already full that week. The "fully booked already" prompt appears with both options.
  6. Hover a stage boundary and open the insert menu: measurement, preprocessing and a stage.
