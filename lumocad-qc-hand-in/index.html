LumoCad QC Hand-In

When the checker or the corrector finishes their part of a quality check in LumoCad, they press Hand in. The building moves to the next step, the next person gets an email, and LumoPlan shows the step as done. When the checker approves, the building goes to the next stage planned in its LumoPlan timeline.

Built and tested · not released yet

Updated 7 October 2026 with Rio's feedback. What changed is listed at the bottom of the page.

Who is involved

Three people meet in this flow. The colors stay the same in every picture below.

Checker

Reviews the 3D model or the 2D plans in LumoCad, ticks the checklists and flags every problem found. When nothing is left, approves and picks who works on the building next.

Corrector

Usually the person in charge of the building. Fixes the problems the checker flagged, then hands the building back for a re-check.

Coordinator

Plans the work in LumoPlan, including which buildings need a room book, and sees which steps are really finished without asking everyone every day.

One QC round, step by step

A QC round goes back and forth between checker and corrector until nothing is left to fix. Each arrow is one press of the Hand in button, and each one sends exactly one email.

In LumoCad the button only lights up when the person's part is complete: for the checker, every checklist is ticked; for the corrector, every flagged issue is resolved. The hand-in window then asks who gets the building next. LumoCad suggests someone sensible, and it never suggests the checker as their own corrector or as the next person after approval.

Every QC step has a pending and an in progress status, the re-check included. The hand-in sets the pending one. When the checker starts, they set it to in progress in the status dropdown, so time spent checking can be told apart from time waiting.

Approving: where the building goes next

Not every building has the same stages after QC. Some need a room book, some go straight to delivery. That is planned in the building's timeline in LumoPlan, so the approve window asks LumoPlan which stage comes next.

  • Pre-selected from the plan. LumoCad asks LumoPlan once, when the window opens. LumoPlan looks at the stages planned after the QC loop and names the first one.
  • The checker can change it. The choices are 2D postprocessing (3D QC only), Roombook and Ready for delivery, plus whatever LumoPlan names.
  • Always works. If LumoPlan can't be reached or has no plan for the building, the window falls back to the usual next stage: 2D postprocessing after 3D QC, Ready for delivery after 2D QC.
  • The checker picks the next person, normally the person in charge of the building. They become the building's assignee and get an email.

Room book in LumoPlan. Room book is now a stage LumoPlan can plan. It is optional: no standard timeline includes it. Coordinators add it for the buildings that need one, per building or through the project type. The plan stays in LumoPlan; the platform only gets the status the checker confirms.

What one button press sets off

The building record on the Lumoview server is the one place everything meets. It keeps a log of every status change and who the building was handed to. The only direct call between the apps is the approve window asking LumoPlan for the next stage.

The four emails

Each email names the QC step and the building, and the building name links straight into LumoCad. One action sends one email. Before this, people got a vague "Work item assigned" or "New building assigned" email, sometimes several at once.

What the coordinator sees in LumoPlan

The postprocessing board shows the plan and which steps are really done. A hand-in in LumoCad ticks the QC step by itself, and the tooltip says who checked and who it went to.

The same applies to 2D. Only the hand-in completes a QC step. LumoPlan can't see the checklists, so on the QC and corrections steps it offers Open checklist in LumoCad instead of Mark done. Other steps, like 3D and 2D postprocessing, keep the manual tick. Ticking only says "done"; it never moves the planned dates.

Fixing a mistake

If someone hands in by mistake, an admin sets the building's status back in the cloud. LumoPlan follows the status log, so the tick disappears by itself and nobody has to undo anything in LumoPlan.

This needed the new "corrections check" status. Before, handing back and undoing a hand-in were the same status change, and moving the status back added a second tick instead of removing one.

Changing the checker later

Sometimes the checker has to change after QC is set up. LumoCad had this feature, but it was switched off because it broke on real buildings. It works again now.

Still open

  • Every stage needing both a pending and an in-progress status makes for many statuses. Parked for a better general solution later, such as one status per stage plus a "started" flag.
  • If an admin undoes a corrector's hand-in by setting the status back to corrections, it looks the same as the checker finding problems again, so LumoPlan counts another QC round.

Before it goes live

  1. Full test run on the test system: set up QC, hand in with problems, hand back, approve into each kind of next stage, change the checker. Check every email and the LumoPlan board.

  2. Release the Lumoview server first. It carries the emails, the new statuses and the "change checker" handling.

  3. Release LumoPlan, then LumoCad. LumoPlan answers the approve window's question and can plan room books. LumoCad's Hand in buttons then go live.

  4. Switch LumoPlan over on the go-live day. One date setting makes QC steps complete only through the hand-in from that day on. Earlier ticks stay as they are.

  5. Tell the team how the round works now, which email to expect at each step, and that room books are planned in LumoPlan.
