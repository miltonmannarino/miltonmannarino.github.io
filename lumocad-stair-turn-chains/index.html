Stairs as a chain of turns, plus sprint 2.2 fixes

Draft · not browser-checked Mergeable 46 files · +14,508 / −794 before merge development merged in · 24ae08fdb riser fix · dc3bb9074 follow-ups · 1700122e7

This PR turns the sprint 2.2 stair feedback from René and Djunejt into code. Any stair is now one ordered list of landings and winders, so shapes that matched no IFC type can be built and saved. It also fixes four bugs from the sprint review. On 16 Oct it was merged with Piotr's multi-slab work.

4 risers→ Landing · straight on→ 5 risers→ Landing · 90° left→ 3 risers→ Winder · 90° left · 3→ 2 risers

No IFC preset matches this example, so it saves as USERDEFINED and the type select reads “Custom turns”. One builder, buildChainGeometry, walks the list. The 20 preset shapes produce the same geometry as before.

1What changed

Turn chain (P1)

  • ST-1Stairs are an ordered list of landings (straight on, 90°, 180°) and winders (90°, 180°). Each turn has its own side. IFC stair types are now presets that fill in this list.
  • The Turns section in the stair panel has an Add menu, a remove button on each turn, and a Turn angle select. A straight stair shows the section too, so landings can be added to it.
  • stairMachine gains turn.add and turn.remove. Changing a turn's kind or angle re-derives the type.
  • On load, the chain is rebuilt from the saved children (winder flights, flights meeting at a landing, headings), not from the stored type. If the stored preset disagrees with the children, it is corrected and flagged.
  • At placement, the side click mirrors the whole chain instead of forcing every turn to one side, so a Z-shape keeps its shape. A Custom turns stair with at least one real turn asks for the side click too.
  • The landing field is labelled “Landing width” for a U-turn landing and “Landing depth” for a straight-on one.
  • Panel numbers show at most one decimal (674.9 mm, 627.1 mm) instead of up to six.
  • When a stair opening does not fit a slab, the message names the floor and slab and says what is wrong, for example “The stair opening reaches past the edge of OG01 · elevationSlab”. It used to show raw ids and a validator message.
  • Changing Number of risers now keeps every turn at the same relative height: a landing after riser 8 of 16 moves to riser 28 of 56. Before, it stayed at riser 8, so a half turn put 48 risers on the return flight and its opening ran 10.8 m behind the start, off the slab. Found while testing on 16 Oct.

Sprint fixes (P0)

  • ST-2The stairwell well width (Treppenauge) now works for half-winding stairs too. Half winders with a well fan around a real eye.
  • ST-5Stairs have their own layer and a Toggle stairs button in the orbit view. Hiding walls no longer hides stairs.
  • ST-6Deleting a stair now removes its slab void. Copying a slab boundary to other floors used to copy stair voids with fresh ids the stair could not find. On delete, the stair also clears matching copies already in saved buildings.
  • ST-11In plan, the path arrow tip sits on the lower break line. Landing paths meet at a corner, and the walking line on winders follows the arc.

Merged with multi-slab (16 Oct)

  • The turn editor and stair panel now use Piotr's stair sidebar components. StairActionButton now forwards a ref so it can open the Add menu.
  • Stairs that span several slabs mirror their turns the same way single-slab stairs do.
  • Copy slabs (copyStoreySlabs) no longer copies stair openings. The target slab keeps its own openings as long as they still fit the new outline.
  • profile.replace.curves follows Piotr's rule: an explicit void list is final. Keeping a slab's own stair voids is opt-in through keepOwnStairVoids, which only the old copy-boundary dialog sets.
  • On delete, the sweep for copied stair voids now checks every slab on each floor.

In the app (CAD mode, stair work mode, a building with two slabbed floors)

  1. Start placing a straight stair. Open Turns and use Add → Landing, straight on twice. the preview shows three flights, and the type select reads “Custom turns” (disabled, not offered as a preset).
  2. Click Add stair, pick Quarter turn, and click the start point and then the direction. Move the mouse to the other side of the line and click there. the preview flips as the mouse crosses the line, and the placed stair turns toward the side you clicked. Once selected, Turns → Turn direction matches.
  3. Click Add stair → Quarter turn → Add → Landing, 90° turn. Set Landing 1 to Left and Landing 2 to Right, then click the start point and the direction. Move the mouse to the other side of the line and click. the type reads “Custom turns”, and the whole Z mirrors to the side you clicked, so it turns right then left. It is still the same Z after a reload.
  4. Drag a stair so it runs past a slab edge on the floor above. the error names the floor and slab (for example “OG01 · elevationSlab”), with no ids.
  5. Select the placed stair and reload the page. the same chain comes back, and the Turns section lists the same landings and winder.
  6. Pick a preset again in Stair type, for example Quarter turn. the custom chain is replaced by the preset's turns.
  7. On a half turn with 16 risers, set Number of risers to 56. the landing stays in the middle (Turns shows it after riser 28) and both flights are equal.
  8. Place a half-winding stair and set Stairwell well width to 200 mm. the winders fan around an open eye with no gap or overlap.
  9. In the orbit view, turn off Toggle walls, then Toggle stairs. stairs stay visible with walls off, and disappear only with their own toggle.
  10. Put a stair through a slab. Use Copy boundary to all levels from the floor with the opening, then delete the stair. no stair opening appears on the other floors, and deleting the stair leaves no void anywhere.
  11. Export a floor plan (DXF/PDF) with a cut stair. the arrow tip touches the lower break line, and landing paths meet at the landing centre.
  12. Multi-slab: place a stair attached to two slabs with a custom chain, then edit its turns. both runs update and the slab openings follow. Nobody has tried this case yet.
