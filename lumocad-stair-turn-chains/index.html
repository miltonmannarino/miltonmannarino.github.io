Stairs as a chain of turns, shaped slab openings and construction types

On development · 3a9e32c8e Platform CI + dev deploy green PR #77 merged 16 Oct PR #100 merged 19 Oct Dev API deployed · types 1.1.73 Production API not updated yet

This work turns the sprint 2.2 stair feedback from René and Djunejt into code. Any stair is now one ordered list of landings and winders, so shapes that matched no IFC type can be built and saved. Slab openings now follow the stair's shape instead of a box around it. Each stair also says how it is built: solid, stepped underside, smooth underside or floating treads.

4 risers→ Landing · straight on→ 5 risers→ Landing · 90° left→ 3 risers→ Winder · 90° left · 3→ 2 risers

No IFC preset matches this example, so it saves as USERDEFINED and the type select reads “Custom turns”. One builder, buildChainGeometry, walks the list. The 20 preset shapes produce the same geometry as before.

1What changed

Turn chain (P1)PR #77 · 16 Oct

  • ST-1Stairs are an ordered list of landings (straight on, 90°, 180°) and winders (90°, 180°). Each turn has its own side. IFC stair types are now presets that fill in this list.
  • The Turns section in the stair panel has an Add menu, a remove button on each turn, and a Turn angle select. A straight stair shows the section too, so landings can be added to it.
  • stairMachine gains turn.add and turn.remove. Changing a turn's kind or angle re-derives the type.
  • On load, the chain is rebuilt from the saved children (winder flights, flights meeting at a landing, headings), not from the stored type. If the stored preset disagrees with the children, it is corrected and flagged.
  • At placement, the side click mirrors the whole chain instead of forcing every turn to one side, so a Z-shape keeps its shape. A Custom turns stair with at least one real turn asks for the side click too.
  • The landing field is labelled “Landing width” for a U-turn landing and “Landing depth” for a straight-on one.

Sprint fixes (P0)PR #77 · 16 Oct

  • ST-2The stairwell well width (Treppenauge) now works for half-winding stairs too. Half winders with a well fan around a real eye.
  • ST-5Stairs have their own layer and a Toggle stairs button in the orbit view. Hiding walls no longer hides stairs.
  • ST-6Deleting a stair removes its slab void. Copying a slab boundary to other floors used to copy stair voids with fresh ids the stair could not find. On delete, the stair also clears matching copies already in saved buildings.
  • ST-11In plan, the path arrow tip sits on the lower break line. Landing paths meet at a corner, and the walking line on winders follows the arc.

Merged with multi-slabPR #77 · 16 Oct

  • The turn editor and stair panel use Piotr's stair sidebar components. StairActionButton forwards a ref so it can open the Add menu.
  • Stairs that span several slabs mirror their turns the same way single-slab stairs do.
  • Copy slabs (copyStoreySlabs) no longer copies stair openings. The target slab keeps its own openings as long as they still fit the new outline.
  • profile.replace.curves follows Piotr's rule: an explicit void list is final. Keeping a slab's own stair voids is opt-in through keepOwnStairVoids, which only the old copy-boundary dialog sets.

Follow-ups that missed PR #77PR #100 · 19 Oct

PR #77 was merged before these three commits were pushed, so they reached development through PR #100.

  • Changing Number of risers keeps every turn at the same relative height: a landing after riser 8 of 16 moves to riser 28 of 56. Before, it stayed at riser 8, so a half turn put 48 risers on the return flight and its opening ran 10.8 m behind the start.
  • Panel numbers show at most one decimal (674.9 mm) instead of up to six. Stored values stay exact.
  • When a stair opening does not fit a slab, the message names the floor and slab, for example “The stair opening reaches past the edge of OG01 · Slab. Move the stair inside the slab.” It used to show raw ids.

Slab openings follow the stair's shapePR #100 · 19 Oct

  • The opening is the union of every flight and landing footprint, plus the stairwell eye between two straight flights that pass each other, over the run they share. It used to be one rectangle around the whole stair.
  • An L or a Z keeps the slab in the corners the stair never reaches. A half turn keeps the floor beside the lower flight where the shorter arriving flight has already stepped off; the old box cut two treads of floor away there.
  • A chain that closes a loop around a stairwell opens the stairwell too, like any eye.
  • On a floor plan, the void cross is one X over a rectangular opening, and one X per piece otherwise, so no diagonal crosses slab that stays.
  • Deleting a stair still finds copied voids in the old rectangular shape. Existing stairs rewrite their opening the next time they are saved.

Smaller fixesPR #100 · 19 Oct

  • Errors from editing a slab under a stair now name the floor as well as the slab.
  • ST-6If the server refuses a stair delete, the stair comes back with its slab openings. Before, it came back without them until its next save.
  • Custom-turn stairs get the landing depth handle in plan. Square 90° landings have none, the handle sits on the edge the depth grows towards, and its arrow points outward. On a mirrored half turn the arrow used to point inward.

Construction type (P2)ST-12 · 19 Oct

  • Each stair has a Construction select in the stair panel, plus a thickness field for every type except solid. The stair decides how it is built; the view-wide “Stairs: solid / open tread” button in the orbit view is gone.
  • The 3D view and the IFC download build the stair from it. Sections and elevations draw a smooth underside as stepped, because those drawings are built from vertical blocks.
  • Winders have no planar underside: on a smooth stair they are drawn stepped.
  • Saved as stairConstructionType on the stair and Pset_StairCommon.waistThickness. Stairs saved before today have neither and load as smooth underside, 150 mm.
  • Released to dev: ifc4-types 1.0.22, types 1.1.73, and the API on development (02bb548), which accepts the two new property names.

Already checked in the browser

19 Oct, local app against the dev API, on the test building with floors UG01, EG00 and OG01. After each change the saved opening was read back from the API.

  • Half winding with a 300 mm well: the opening keeps a 270 mm strip of floor beside the lower flight's first tread, visible in the top view.
  • Quarter turn (L): the opening is an L, and the inner corner of 1,890 × 1,890 mm stays slab.
  • Custom chain (L plus a straight landing): the type reads “Custom turns”, the opening follows the longer flight, and dragging the straight landing's handle changed its depth from 1,000 to 1,396.9 mm and saved.

In the app (CAD mode, CAD: Stair, a building with two slabbed floors)

  1. Select a stair. In the stair panel, switch Construction through Solid, Stepped underside, Smooth underside and Floating treads, with the orbit view in geometry mode. the 3D stair changes with each choice, and the thickness field appears for every type except Solid. Its label reads “Tread thickness” for floating.
  2. Set Smooth underside, change the waist thickness to 250 mm, and reload the page. the stair comes back as Smooth underside with 250 mm.
  3. Open a stair that was saved before 19 Oct. it shows Smooth underside with 150 mm and loads without errors.
  4. Download the IFC model with a smooth stair and a floating stair in it. the exported stairs look like the 3D view: a sloped underside and loose treads.
  5. Generate a section through a smooth stair. the stair is drawn with a stepped underside.
  6. Place a quarter turn (L) through a slab, then look at the slab on the floor above in the top view and in 3D. the opening is L-shaped, and the inner corner of the L is still slab. Top view checked 19 Oct · 3D not yet
  7. Export a floor plan of a floor the stair passes through. the void cross has one X per rectangle of the L, not one X across the whole box.
  8. Start placing a straight stair. Open Turns and use Add → Landing, straight on twice. the preview shows three flights, and the type select reads “Custom turns” (disabled, not offered as a preset).
  9. On a custom stair with a straight-on landing, drag the landing's arrow in the top view. the arrow points away from the landing, the depth follows the mouse, and the panel's Landing depth updates. Checked 19 Oct
  10. Click Add stair → Quarter turn → Add → Landing, 90° turn. Set Landing 1 to Left and Landing 2 to Right, then click the start point and the direction. Move the mouse to the other side of the line and click. the type reads “Custom turns”, and the whole Z mirrors to the side you clicked. It is still the same Z after a reload.
  11. On a half turn with 16 risers, set Number of risers to 56. the landing stays in the middle (Turns shows it after riser 28) and both flights are equal.
  12. Drag a stair so it runs past a slab edge on the floor above, then also try moving that slab's edge under a stair. both errors name the floor and slab (for example “OG01 · Slab”), with no ids.
  13. Place a half-winding stair and set Stairwell well width to 200 mm. the winders fan around an open eye with no gap or overlap.
  14. In the orbit view, turn off Toggle walls, then Toggle stairs. stairs stay visible with walls off, and disappear only with their own toggle.
  15. Put a stair through a slab. Use Copy boundary to all levels from the floor with the opening, then delete the stair. no stair opening appears on the other floors, and deleting the stair leaves no void anywhere.
  16. Multi-slab: place a stair attached to two slabs with a custom chain, then edit its turns. both runs update and the slab openings follow. Nobody has tried this case yet.
