The opening placement guide, end to end

How captured doors and windows get from the surveyor's records onto the right wall: what the computer proposes, what you check, and what only a person may confirm.

Repository Lumoview-Building-Analytics-GmbH/platform Branch agentic-dev-milton (work not yet committed) As of 10 November 2026 Verified on Hohental Pilot Osnabrück [Milton], Level 1 (OG01)

In one paragraph. The surveyor captures each door and window as a record with its size and the rooms it connects, but not its position on a wall. The 360 photos carry AI masks that outline doors and windows. The placement engine combines the two: it finds the wall between the linked rooms where the masks say the opening is, then runs safety checks. Openings that pass become positions to check. The guide walks you through them one by one, with Top View and the 360 photo both showing the same blue proposal. Nothing is saved until you confirm it in LumoCad's own Attach Door or Attach Window dialog.

On the test level, the guide first showed 0 positions to check. The engine had in fact found 10 of 11 positions, and all were blocked only because the finished rooms were locked. After one click on Unlock rooms and show positions, there were 9 proposals, plus a 10th marked by hand.

The workflow at a glance

Using it, step by step

  1. Open the level in the right mode. Pick the level, switch the left rail to CAD: Structure, and keep Top View and 360 Image open. The guide arranges the two side by side.
  2. Press Review openings at the top of the right sidebar. The guide reads the photo masks for the level. It then lists captured openings, positions to check and needs information. Photo detections that aren't linked to a captured record are counted separately; they are not extra openings.
  3. If a yellow banner appears, unlock first. It reads something like 10 openings have a position, but their rooms are locked. Finished rooms are locked against edits, so their positions can be neither shown nor placed. Unlock rooms and show positions does the same as the room padlock in the left sidebar. It changes editor state only and saves nothing, then recalculates.
  4. Check the numbered proposal in both views. Each opening gets a stable label: D01, D02… for doors and W01, W02… for windows. Both cameras move to it. Top View frames the proposed opening on its wall (Proposed W05 · Wall 13), and the 360 photo shows a blue frame over the opening. Compare the blue frame with the real door or window in the photo.
  5. Confirm or adjust. Review placement opens LumoCad's Attach Door or Attach Window dialog, prefilled with the captured size. Attach saves; Cancel leaves everything as it was. If the position is slightly off, press Adjust frame and mark the frame yourself (step 7).
  6. Press Next opening. After a save the guide stays on the same opening and shows Placement saved. It moves on only when you ask, so the camera never jumps on its own. When the list is done it says No more positions to check and points to the records that still need information.
  7. For an opening without a proposal, mark its frame. Choose it under Needs information and press Mark door frame or Mark window frame. Both views first go to a photo that sees the opening from a useful distance. Then:
    • Step 1 of 2: click one vertical edge of the frame, where the fixed frame meets the wall, not the swinging door. A blue line appears where you clicked. Dragging still turns the view.
    • Step 2 of 2: click the opposite edge. A draft position is calculated. If it fits, the opening joins positions to check and both views show it. If not, the box stays open and says why, for example that the wall doesn't join the linked rooms.
  8. Other help actions. Enter dimensions opens a form when the captured width is missing. Show in both views takes the cameras to a help record's photo and wall without starting anything. Records whose room outline is missing tell you to fix the outline in the normal editor; they never ask you to draw an invented one.

What "nothing is saved" means here. Browsing, unlocking rooms, choosing photos, changing the suggestion setting, marking a frame and creating a draft never write to the building. The only write is the native attach dialog, and its normal Undo still applies. The guide's own Undo last placement goes through that same editor Undo.

What happens under the hood

1. Reading the level

For the active level, the guide collects walls, room outlines, captured door and window records, and every horizontal 360 scan. For each scan it takes the position, height and rotation, plus the door and window masks and the photo's alignment angle. Openings already attached to a wall count as occupied space.

2. From mask to wall

The left and right edges of a mask become two rays from the camera. Where the rays hit a wall between the opening's linked rooms gives an observed width and centre. A fit counts when the observed width is close enough to the captured width. If several photos see the same opening, their centres must agree.

3. Safety checks

  • The opening stays within the wall.
  • The wall really joins the captured rooms.
  • No linked room is locked.
  • An aligned photo has a clear view of both ends.
  • It doesn't overlap another opening.

The first check that fails becomes the reason shown under Advanced → Why this result?

4. Sorting into lists

Each record lands in exactly one list: positions to check, needs information (with one next action), placed or set aside. The suggestion setting under Advanced can show weaker, tentative positions. It never lets an unsafe one through.

How the two views follow the selected opening

Top View gets a "frame this area" command right away. If its camera isn't ready yet, the view keeps the command until it is. The 360 photo switches through the editor's normal photo selection. First it leaves a room scope that would hide the photo; then it waits until the photo is actually shown before turning the camera.

For a proposal, the camera aims at all four corners of the opening and zooms to fit them. For a help record, it prefers the aligned photo 1.5 to 7 m away, aims nearly level (between 20° down and 10° up), and uses a 100° view, so the vertical frame edges you need to click are visible. Choosing another opening, closing the guide or picking another photo cancels any pending move.

What changed

Guide and help records

  • Locked rooms are named, not hidden. A position blocked only by a locked room used to be shown as "No safe position found, mark the frame", which was a dead end. It now says which rooms are locked, and one banner unlocks them all and recalculates.
  • Help records can be shown. Show in both views, Mark … frame and the repair actions first bring both views to the record's photo and wall. Merely selecting a help record still moves nothing, as the signed spec requires.
  • Wording: "window" for windows; clearer step texts; the wall picker says it is highlighted in Top View.

Both views follow the selection

  • Top View moves at once, instead of waiting for the photo switch, which could silently fail.
  • The 360 photo switches through the editor's normal selection. It leaves a foreign room scope first, and says plainly when a drawing tool blocks the switch.
  • Help views are usable. The guide picks a photo at a useful distance with a level, wide view. It no longer points at the floor from a photo standing in the doorway.

Frame marking in the 360 photo

  • Clicks register. They are read on the 360 view itself, so no 3D overlay in front of the photo can swallow them. Previously every click was dropped.
  • Blue lines are visible. The 360 photo is drawn as see-through, so solid lines were painted over by the photo. The guide's lines are now see-through too, at full opacity. This also made the guide's blue proposal outline visible in the live app.
  • Crosshair cursor, Step 1 of 2 and Step 2 of 2 texts, a blue line per clicked side, and the failure reason shown in the box.

Testing support

  • Reset doors and windows in Settings → Building backups. It writes a safety backup, deletes doors and windows drawn in LumoCad, and takes captured ones off their walls, back to capture state. Everything else stays. Restore full model of that safety backup undoes it.
  • The placement engine now places 12 of 17 Hohental openings within 50 mm, up from 8 (commit cc7ef5f03).

How to test

In the app

  1. Open Hohental Pilot Osnabrück [Milton] → Level 1 → CAD: Structure.
  2. Press Review openings. Expect 11 captured openings and the yellow banner about 10 positions in locked rooms.
  3. Press Unlock 6 rooms and show positions. Expect 9 positions to check, starting at W05 in Bad, with blue proposals in both views.
  4. Press Next opening through the list: W05, D04, W02, W04, D06, W01, W03, D02, D03. Each should show Proposed … · Wall n in Top View and a blue frame on the real opening in the photo.
  5. Press Review placement on any one. Expect the Attach Door or Attach Window dialog prefilled with the captured size. Cancel should leave the guide on the same opening with no "Placement saved".
  6. Under Needs information, pick D05 and press Mark door frame. A corridor photo opens. Click the left, then the right edge of the closed white door's frame. Expect a blue line after the first click, then D05 joins the positions to check.
  7. Pick D01 and press Enter dimensions. Expect a width, height and sill form.

All of the above was run on 10 November 2026 in a separate test browser on that building, with screenshots at every step. Only Cancel was pressed in the attach dialog, so nothing was saved.

Glossary
  • Captured opening: a door or window recorded on site with size and linked rooms, but no wall position.
  • Mask: the AI outline of a door or window in a 360 photo.
  • Aligned photo: a 360 photo whose rotation is known relative to the plan. Only aligned photos count as evidence.
  • Proposal: a position that passed every safety check. It is drawn in blue and is never saved on its own.
  • Draft: a proposal made from your two frame clicks. It is checked like any other proposal.
  • Locked room: a finished room protected against edits. You unlock it with the padlock in the left sidebar or with the guide's banner.
