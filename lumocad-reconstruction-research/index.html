Lumoview · building reconstruction research

From scan evidence
to editable buildings.

Use measured geometry to anchor the model, panoramas to recognize and connect its parts, and agents to orchestrate proposals that can be checked.

The strongest near-term opportunity is existing panorama masks → calibrated rays → independently measured walls → hosted doors and windows. In parallel, improve candidate search around the existing room graph. New structured models should compete under the same evaluator.

Project: Lumoview-Building-Analytics-GmbH/platform · Findings as of 7 November 2026 · Research recommendations, not demonstrated product performance.

01 / Acquisition before algorithms

Our input is richer—and sparser—than a generic 3D scan.

Measured evidence

Raw RotLidarData includes horizontal angle/range rings and vertical slices, plus orientation information. Available paired panoramas, capture-time opening dimensions and room connections add semantic and topological constraints.

A horizontal ring constrains the plan at its sampled height. It cannot observe an entire window, sill and lintel.

Derived evidence

The dense coloured room cloud is a separate reconstruction product. Masks, tagged points and visual clouds may share the same panorama source. Their agreement is useful, but does not count as independent sensor confirmation.

The frontend reconstruction contract uses millimetres, Z-up and radians. Preserve the complete frame, unit and calibration chain.

Do not extrude a sparse ring into a “dense scan” and treat the extra points as observations. Evaluate dense-cloud methods on the derived cloud in a separate lane.

Local contract: 3D point-cloud data and panorama mask assets (repository access required).

02 / Recommended architecture

A common evaluator is more durable than one winning model.

The current branch already contains bounded SE(2) joint room registration, door-guided candidate seeds, relationship safeguards and measured/inferred wall classifications. Its local graph jointly evaluates constructed supported edges; it does not search every possible room relation. New methods should improve this baseline.

Keep image masks and generated scene descriptions as data. Convert candidates through a strict schema and existing planner. A model must never execute generated geometry code or become a parallel database writer.

03 / Best first experiment

Turn recognizable openings into metric, hosted objects.

  1. Inspect the existing evidence.

    Evaluate original objectTags masks and queued observation output. Retain source spherical masks and capture dimensions. A rendering overlay or mask centroid loses useful boundary information.

  2. Project with a complete calibration chain.

    Use overlapping perspective crops for general detectors, retain crop rotations, merge at the panorama seam, then recover calibrated rays. Range along a spherical ray differs from perspective camera Z-depth.

  3. Fit competing wall hosts independently.

    Use measured wall lines/faces, not the current CAD outline as proof. Fit along-wall position, width, sill and lintel where observed. Treat repeated doors as alternative identity assignments.

  4. Verify visibility, identity and dimensions.

    A nearer measured occluder blocks a far host. An open door reveals a background wall; that wall must not become its host. A closed leaf, frame, rough opening and clear aperture are different targets.

  5. Propose the exact native edit.

    Return source IDs, host alternatives, uncertainty and unresolved fields. Swing, facing, panels and transoms need their own evidence or review. Preserve approval, save confirmation and Undo.

Circularity trap. Some observation world positions are intersections with the current room footprint, with a fallback when no hit exists. They cannot independently validate that footprint. The current opening-evidence path already recovers original rays and avoids cached fallback positions; extend that safeguard.

Calibration error sets a practical floor.

For a target 5 metres away, even a small angular error can dominate fine coordinate snapping. This is an illustrative geometric calculation, not a measured scanner error.

87 mm

lateral offset = 5,000 mm × tan(yaw error)

For ray-plane intersection, a near-grazing ray makes the denominator approach zero. Reject unstable intersections and propagate pose uncertainty rather than implying millimetre precision.

Useful implementations: py360convert, Grounding DINO, SAM 2. These recognize/project evidence; they do not establish architectural aperture dimensions by themselves.

04 / Assembly and wall geometry

Room adjacency is not the same problem as cloud overlap.

Opposite wall faces should not coincide.

Two rooms often see opposite interior faces of one wall, separated by thickness. Whole-room overlap maximization can collapse them together. Match genuine shared observations separately from opposing parallel surfaces.

A door graph tells us which rooms connect. It does not uniquely determine metric pose or the host wall. Repeated rooms and corridors can support several plausible assemblies.

Retain weak and missing constraints.

Parallel corridor walls poorly constrain translation along the corridor. A low fitting residual is insufficient. Keep alternatives, conditioning diagnostics and disconnected components explicit.

Known gravity helps reduce the problem to planar pose; a Manhattan building is a separate assumption. Preserve supported diagonal walls, recesses, holes and local direction families.

SALVe offers the closest semantic assembly precedent: generate aperture correspondences, verify relative layouts, then optimize a graph. Its missing released door/window frontend prevents treating it as turnkey. Our existing masks and metric measurements make an adapted version worth testing.

Storeys need independent vertical evidence. Derive levels from sections, distinguish finish floor from structural slab/ceiling, and use stable exterior/stair-core references for XY/yaw. Copied floor layouts are proposals, checked against destination measurements. These practical requirements come from the Revit workflow.

Exterior thickness remains underdetermined from an interior face alone. Use measured reveals, captured thickness or a verified exterior mesh. Otherwise classify the outside face as inferred. Model one physical shared wall with its observed faces and hosted openings.

06 / Recent papers that change the design conversation

Borrow representations before committing to a training stack.

CRAB: editable components and relations.

The October 2026 paper represents walls/openings as volumetric components with adjacency and host relations. Its local fixed/editable correction idea aligns well with native building objects. Its thickness result is on matched synthetic walls, not demonstrated real survey accuracy. A usable code/checkpoint/license release was not verified. Primary paper.

MvFFN: persistent wall identity across panoramas.

Also from October 2026, it predicts local ordered wall instances and chains them deterministically. The useful idea is consistent physical wall identity across views. Straightened panoramas, adequate overlap and relative camera heights are assumptions; code is promised on acceptance. Primary paper.

MapAnything: use the information we already measured.

Optional poses, calibration and depth make conditioned geometry a more relevant trial than discarding reliable measurements for image-only reconstruction. Select the specific Apache checkpoint. Ray-direction inputs do not by themselves prove accurate native equirectangular inference: begin with calibrated perspective crops. Official implementation.

BUFFER-X and RAP: newer dense registration comparisons.

BUFFER-X targets descriptor transfer; RAP explores multiway generative registration. Test them after transparent dense baselines. A generated cloud must not replace the original measured points used to validate the recovered rigid transforms. BUFFER-X · RAP.

A good layout score can still produce expensive modelling work.

No-return is not free space. Glass, dark materials, occlusion and range limits all matter. Count a beam contradiction only where a valid measured ray traversed the relevant space.

Freeze entire held-out buildings. Do not align each predicted room independently to ground truth: that erases puzzle errors. Do not optimize scale against accepted geometry in a metric-scanner evaluation. Same-scan withheld points are useful engineering checks, but remain correlated.

Report median/P95 absolute wall-face distance and signed bias separately; missed/extra wall length; wrong room merges; opening centre/size/sill error; host/identity mistakes; inference coverage; time to accepted output. Agree tolerances with QC before the trial. Existing code thresholds are not survey certification.

08 / Concrete research backlog

Six trials, with a shared decision rule.

P0

Freeze evidence and calibration.

Build a small permissioned corpus spanning repeated rooms, glass, clutter, diagonals, closed doors and level changes. Preserve original rings/slices, panoramas, capture dimensions, masks, derived clouds and accepted references.

Gate: known-point projection roundtrips; complete units/frames/revisions; unavailable and inferred evidence explicit. Pilot size is not a statistical guarantee.

P1

Existing masks → hosted opening rectangles.

Keep the detector fixed. Compare current placement against ray/host competition and dimension-constrained fitting. Then test Grounding DINO + SAM2 or other masks only if recognition is the measured bottleneck.

Measure: wrong host/identity, duplicates, position/size/sill errors, proposal coverage and correction minutes.

P2

Same-score planar search.

Compare seeded differential evolution with correlative branch-and-bound under identical score, point budget, resolution and movement limits. Feed candidates into the native bounded graph.

Measure: top-k pose recall, false merges, degeneracy, runtime and independent measurement agreement. Verify bounds on an exhaustively searchable small domain.

P3

Independent geometry audit.

Extend finite-patch checks with complete withheld captures and valid measured-beam contradiction tests. Preserve unknown regions and provenance dependencies.

Measure: false acceptance and usable coverage. If no independent capture remains, state that limitation explicitly.

P4

Native fitter versus structured proposals.

Compare RoomFormer with the existing line-arrangement outline fitter. Separate actual ring/slice inputs from derived dense-cloud inputs; preserve diagonals and shared walls.

Measure: metric errors, missed/extra wall length, adjacency and false closures, plus edits needed for an accepted plan.

P5

Conditioned geometry and selective dense registration.

Audit the current worker/checkpoint, then compare MapAnything Apache with identical calibrated crops and trusted pose/range inputs. If dense registration is needed, start with Open3D/small_gicp/KISS-Matcher before BUFFER-X/RAP.

Measure: withheld range, scale/pose and downstream opening errors, memory/runtime/cost. Visual cloud quality alone cannot pass.

The decision rule: at a tolerable rate of wrong proposals, does the method complete more work and reduce time to an accepted model?

P0 precedes accuracy claims. P1 and P2 can run independently; P3 evaluates later trials. Pin commits, checkpoints, preprocessing, seeds, hardware, bounds, source/holdout IDs, metrics and correction records.

11 / Evidence and boundaries

What this investigation establishes.

Verified: relevant local contracts and code surfaces; primary publications; visible code/model availability; the specific license and evaluator distinctions cited above. Inferred: experiment order, fusion architecture and expected integration value. Unmeasured: accuracy, runtime, calibration distributions and labour savings on Lumoview captures.

The investigation read relevant parts of platform, API, Lumoagent, Lumoflow and shared types. It did not inspect every company repository, remote branch or external worker. No model weights were downloaded, paid inference run, customer building changed, or reconstruction benchmark executed.

Starting context: LumoCad agent workforce audit and Revit production workflow. Their checked-out HTML was read when the web reader could not fetch the live pages. Historical audit statements were checked against current local code; they are not assumed current.

Terms used on this page

SE(2): rigid planar motion: x, y and yaw. BEV: bird’s-eye raster projection. ERP: equirectangular panorama. Host: the wall that contains an opening. Gauge: arbitrary global coordinates removed by fixing an anchor. Abstention: declining a proposal when evidence is insufficient. Independent evidence: measurements whose agreement was not created by the candidate being tested.
