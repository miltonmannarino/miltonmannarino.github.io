Three weeks in LumoPlan: what you will notice

Twelve releases went to the live app at plan.lumoview.com in these three weeks. This page walks through them by the place where you see them: the post-processing board, the dashboards, hour logging, the team view, offers and tickets. A short section at the end covers LumoCad, the 3D modelling tool, where some of the same weeks went.

State on 5 November 2026. Everything in the LumoPlan sections is live in production. The three releases of 4 November passed the automated checks but have not yet been clicked through by hand on the live board. Two things are paused on purpose: stage reminders by e-mail, and the hour-planning trial.

Post-processing boardWhere: the big planning timeline on the Postprocessing page, in Planning mode.

The board is where coordinators plan who works on which building and stage on which days. It got the most changes, and the direction is simple: the board says who and when; the hours people log afterwards say how long it really took.

4 Nov

Assign anyone, hours do not blocklive

Right-clicking a stage to pick a person, painting days with the brush, and dragging a stage onto other days no longer refuse or skip a day because the person is already "full". There is no more prompt about capacity. Days when the person is absent or does not work are still skipped, and dropping a stage on a holiday still asks. The "Cleanup Overbooking" entry in the ⋮ menu was removed, because with overlaps allowed one click would have deleted real bookings.

Try it: right-click a stage, pick someone who is already booked that week. They are assigned with no dialog.
4 Nov

Hour numbers leave the timelinelive

Stage bars, person blocks and their hover text no longer show planned hours. Stages shorter than a day keep their real length instead of being stretched. The hours are still stored, so Tasks and the team views keep their numbers.

16 Oct

Milestones on a building's rowlive

A building can carry dated milestones, such as a client meeting or a hand-over. They show as small pills along the bottom edge of the building's row. Clicking a pill ticks it done; a milestone past its date turns red. Edit Timeline has a Milestones list to add and remove them, and My Work shows a "My milestones" card.

Row bottom edge✓ Kick-off 14 OctClient review 2 NovHand-over 20 Nov
Try it: open Edit Timeline on a building, add a milestone with yesterday's date. It appears red on the row; click it and it turns green.
16 Oct

Packing Sports as one assigneelive

Packing Sports is the external team that does preprocessing. It can now be put on a preprocessing stage as one group, like a person. The group has no capacity limit, gets no assignment e-mail, and stays out of the people pickers for post-processing stages, where it would not belong.

Still missing: the group cannot be a building's main assignee yet; the row picker lists people only.

30 Oct

Delivered can be marked donelive

The Delivered stage now has "Mark stage done" and "Reopen stage" in its right-click menu, exactly like 3D Postprocessing through Ready for Delivery. It also works from My Work and the building page. Client revisions and Invoiced are unchanged.

Try it: right-click a Delivered bar › Mark stage done. The bar shows a ✓ in full colour.
19 Oct

Folding a project is yours alonelive

Opening or closing a project on the board used to change everyone's board. Now the open or closed state lives in your own browser, and every project starts closed on a new day. Nobody can fold your view away any more.

19 Oct

Bulk edit reaches preprocessinglive

With several buildings selected, dragging a preprocessing end, the pending/in-progress split, or the start of the first post-processing stage now offers to apply the same change to the other selected buildings. "Overwrite all stages" no longer resets their preprocessing split.

Dashboards: building, project, OperationsWhere: a building's page, a project's page, the Projects list, and the Operations page.

These were Rio's two Q4 tickets: see the real working hours and cost per building, see what each project actually sold, and track per building whether the 3D model was built in LumoCad or in Revit.

19 Oct

Building page: hours, BGF/h and invoicedlive

Each stage (measurement, preprocessing, every post-processing stage) now shows the hours people actually logged on it, with start, due date and booked days, in date order. Three new cards at the top: worked hours, BGF per hour (gross floor area modelled per hour of work), and invoiced amount from sent and paid invoices. The page also states the building's category, meaning the model level and outputs sold in the offer, and its LumoCad record.

Try it: open a delivered building. The stage table has a Worked h column and the three cards sit above it.
19 Oct

Projects: Product column and category groupinglive

The Projects list and each project page show a Product: the model level and outputs sold, read from the project's accepted offers. The building table on a project page has Worked h, BGF/h and Category columns and can be grouped by category, with totals per group for BGF/h, cost per m² and price per m².

Older projects whose offer is only a PDF read "No offer". A manual override is not built.

29 Oct

Progress percentage removedlive

The stage-based progress percentage is gone from the project buildings table and from the pipeline bar, as Rio asked on 28 October. It counted stages, not work, and misled more than it helped.

19 Oct

Operations › Productivitylive

Buildings grouped by category, with BGF/h, cost and price per m², hours per stage and size bands. Only buildings with complete hours count in the ratios: delivered, modelling started after hour logging went live (28 July), and at least half of the booked person-days logged. That keeps half-logged buildings from dragging the averages.

19 and 29 Oct

Operations › Adoptionlive

Per building: modelled in LumoCad, started in LumoCad then switched to Revit, or Revit only, with the reason for a switch and a "should have been LumoCad" flag. A record can be created before modelling starts, with "Planned for LumoCad?" asked up front. Buildings can be set in bulk, and the tab shows totals by project and by person plus a feedback list, laid out like Rio's adoption sheet. Since 29 October it counts only buildings created from 28 October on; a date field and a "Show all buildings" switch bring the older ones back.

Try it: Operations › Adoption, set one building's tool, bulk-set two, then flip "Show all buildings".

Hour loggingWhere: the hour log dialog, reached from the stage menu, My Work or the Tasks page.

4 Nov

Every hour entry names a buildinglive

Hours could be logged on a project without a building. Those hours never reached a building page. In a dashboard review with Rio, Böckhstrasse 47-48 showed only 1.3 h of measurement, while the measuring had been logged on the project. Across the team, most buildings with post-processing hours had no measurement hours at all for this reason. Now the form asks for the building every time; the project-only tab is gone. Old project-level entries stay exactly as they were and can still be saved.

Side effect to tell the team: a measuring trip over several buildings is split by hand, and work on a project with no building yet goes under Internal work.

Team viewWhere: Tasks › Team.

29 Oct

Day, Week and Month zoomlive

Rio wanted one screenshot of the whole team's plan through the end of January. The Team tab now has Day, Week and Month zoom. Week shows up to the end of the third month ahead, Month to the sixth, both fitted to the width of the screen with ISO weeks on the axis. The tab uses the full page width, and the zoom you pick is remembered in your browser.

Try it: Tasks › Team › Month. The whole plan to spring fits on one screen.

Preprocessing overviewWhere: the Preprocessing page, Team and Buildings tabs.

19 Oct

Team tab opens at oncelive

The Team tab used to sit behind one spinner until everything had loaded. Now the person cards appear immediately and fill in activity, building and counts as they arrive. It loads only the last seven days of edits and skips data it never showed. The Buildings tab says what it is loading (the team's edits of the last 45 days) while you wait. The computed stages no longer blank out and come back when the building list grows.

Offers, invoices and ticketsWhere: an offer's page, the invoice editor, and Create Work Item.

4 Nov

Offer services stop re-ticking themselveslive

Entering the Brutto-Grundfläche in an open offer adds the two optional area rows (08a and 08b) unticked, so they stay out of the price until someone picks them. Before, they were added ticked and the "(optional)" label vanished on the next load. Ticking or unticking a service now saves right away; other edits save within 20 seconds and when you leave the offer. Leaving within those 20 seconds no longer loses the change.

Try it: untick a service, switch straight to another offer, come back. Still unticked.
20 Oct

Invoices cite the offer by numberlive

New invoice drafts say "unser Angebot Offer-DE-2026-05-0001 vom …" instead of the whole offer title with date and customer name. The remark in Notion uses the same short number. Invoices drafted earlier keep their saved sentence.

4 Nov

Work items from people outside Notionlive

Create Work Item failed when the reporter's e-mail had no Notion account. Now the ticket is created anyway, with the reporter's name and e-mail in the ticket body and the Upvote field left empty.

29 Oct

Legacy offer linked to its projectdone once

A script links an old offer, made before projects existed in LumoPlan, to an existing project. It runs as a dry run first. It was used once in production, for the Petra Emmer offer and its project.

Also in these weeks: LumoCad and the serverLumoCad is the 3D modelling tool in the Lumoview platform. The server is the shared backend both apps talk to.

Not LumoPlan, but part of the same three weeks. The links go to pages written for each piece.

Live in LumoCad

  • Stairs (19 Oct): each stair has a construction type (solid, stepped and so on), the opening in the floor slab follows the stair's shape, and a custom chain of flights gets a landing handle. Stairs and roofs status.
  • Floor plan sheets (27 Oct): one marketing crop per flat in one step, cleaner elevations, no north arrow on sections and elevations, balconies counted at half their area in the Wohnfläche, door widths left off the generated plan, a readable title block on every sheet type.
  • QC hand-in (28 Oct): a checker can hand in a quality check before the building's status has caught up. The QC e-mails from the server name who assigned, handed in or approved (20 Oct). QC hand-in page.
  • Security updates (26 Oct), same as in LumoPlan.

Built, not yet releasedlocal only

  • Roofs from drone scans (29 Oct): roof surfaces are read off a cleaned-up drone mesh, and roof elements such as dormers can span several faces. Committed locally, not on the shared branch.
  • Airteam drone sync (4 Nov): when Airteam delivers a drone survey, its 3D model reaches the right building in LumoCad without anyone uploading it. Clear name matches link themselves; the rest wait in a review list where a person picks the building. Server and app parts both built, not deployed. Airteam page.

Paused and still openSo nobody is surprised when they look for these.
