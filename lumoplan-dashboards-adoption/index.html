Dashboards and LumoCad adoption, first version

Rio's two Q4 tickets: a dashboard per building with real working hours per stage, a pricing view grouped by what the offer sells, and per-building tracking of whether the model was built in LumoCad or Revit. Everything below is on plan.lumoview.com since 19 Oct.

Live

  • Building page rebuilt along Rio's annotations.
  • Project page groups buildings by offer category with pricing figures.
  • Two new Operations tabs: Productivity and Adoption, with Rio's sheet columns and a Team table.
  • Shipped 19 Oct in PR #55 and #56, deploys green. Type check clean, 2,127 unit tests and the E2E page checks green.

changedWhat changed

Building page

Every annotation on Rio's screenshot is in:

  • Stage table shows worked hours per stage from hour tracking, with draft hours as a hint underneath. Planned hours are gone. Dates are split into Start and Due, where Due is the last planned day.
  • Colour dot per stage, the same colour the postprocessing timeline uses.
  • Rows sorted by date, so a follow-up measurement sits where it happened instead of at the bottom.
  • New cards: Worked hours (all phases, split on hover), BGF/h and Invoiced. Invoiced sums sent and paid invoices, splits a shared invoice by area and deducts partial invoices once.
  • Cost cards explain themselves on hover: hours × the hourly rate per phase from Settings, no overhead.
  • Category in the header, and a Modelling tool card at the bottom with a Set button.

Project page

  • Group buildings by Category (level + output) or Category + size.
  • Category comes from the offer. Level 1 is positions 02/05, Level 2 is 03/06. Outputs are 3D, 2D, sections 08a, elevations 08b and roombook 09a, so a group reads like "Level 1 · 2D + Roombook".
  • Each group header and the total row show worked hours, BGF/h, internal cost per m² and price per m². These are Rio's Notion columns.
  • New columns Worked h, BGF/h and Category, also in the CSV export.
  • The Projects list and the project page show the Product sold, read from the accepted offers (model level plus outputs). On production 3 of 12 open projects have one; the rest read "No offer" because their offer predates LumoPlan.

Operations › Productivity

  • The pricing table across every project: one row per category, with hours split by phase, BGF, BGF/h, cost and price per m², and measurement hours.
  • Open a category to see hours per stage and the buildings behind the numbers.
  • A project filter, an optional size split, and an option to list buildings without hours.

Only complete hours count. Hour logging went live on 28 Jul, so most buildings have partial hours. On dev data they read 100 to 480 m²/h, against about 20 in Rio's sheet. A building enters the ratios only when three things hold:

  • It is delivered.
  • Its preprocessing or postprocessing started on or after 28 Jul.
  • Its logged hours cover at least half of the person-days booked on the board, at 8h each.

Partial buildings carry a badge and can be counted on request. On the building page, BGF/h says "partial".

Operations › Adoption

Built to match the two tabs of Rio's draft sheet, Projects and Team.

  • One record per building: planned for LumoCad or not, and modelled in LumoCad, started in LumoCad and finished in Revit, Revit, or not started yet. A record can exist before modelling starts.
  • Leaving a LumoCad plan needs two answers: "should it have been LumoCad?" (yes, no because too complex, or not sure) and a reason from a fixed list. A switch can also name the step it happened at. A building planned for Revit and done in Revit is asked nothing more.
  • Status and completion date (planned, in progress, completed) come from LumoPlan's own data, nobody types them. Cancelled buildings do not count.
  • Feedback: every record asks for LumoCad feedback, and the tab lists it, so nobody has to chase it in Notion.
  • Cards as in the sheet: tracked, not tracked, intended for LumoCad, finished in LumoCad, switched to Revit, finished / intended, switched / intended, avoidable against too complex. Breakdown by project and a chart of switch reasons.
  • Team table: one row per internal team member, including people who never touched LumoCad. Uses LumoCad and finished a building in LumoCad are read from the building records; uses independently and a note are typed in per person; current projects and buildings come from the bookings.
  • Bulk-set: tick buildings, or the header box for all shown, and save once. Sixty buildings are one request.

Under the hood

  • Stage rows, categories, productivity, invoiced amounts and adoption figures are pure functions with unit tests.
  • New collections buildingModelling and modellerAdoption, routes under /server/.
  • Categories also read LumoPlan's own offers, by building or through the project an accepted offer created. The platform offer API alone had no building links.
  • Buildings without a project are left out of the adoption figures.

testHow to test

1 · Building page

  1. Projects, pick a project, click a building with logged hours.
  2. Stage table: every row has a colour dot matching the timeline, Start and Due columns, and no Planned hours. Rows run in date order; hours logged on a stage the plan no longer has show at the end without dates.
  3. Hover Worked hours: measurement, preprocessing and postprocessing split, plus drafts.
  4. Hover Actual cost: the formula with the rates from Settings.
  5. For a building with a sent invoice, Invoiced shows the net amount.

2 · Project page

  1. Open a project with several buildings and set Group by to Category.
  2. Groups read like "Level 2 · 3D + Roombook", Level 1 first; the header shows worked hours, and m²/h, cost and price once a building has complete hours.
  3. Switch to Category + size: groups split by size band.
  4. Export CSV: Worked hours, BGF/h, Category and Size are in the file.

3 · Operations › Productivity

  1. Open the tab. With few complete buildings a yellow note explains why nothing counts.
  2. Tick Count partial hours: figures appear, partial buildings carry a badge.
  3. Open a category: hours per stage and the buildings, each linking to its page.

4 · Operations › Adoption

  1. Click a building row. The Modelling tool dialog opens; under Modelled in pick Revit, then Planned for LumoCad? Yes. Save stays disabled until "should it have been LumoCad?" and a reason are set.
  2. Switch the plan to No, Revit is the plan. The two questions disappear and Save is enabled.
  3. Save: the cards, the project breakdown, the reasons chart and the feedback list update.
  4. Tick two or more buildings, Set modelling tool, LumoCad, Save. "Saved for N buildings", selection cleared.
  5. Team table: a person who modelled one of those buildings now reads Uses LumoCad. Click a row to set Uses independently and a note.
  6. Open a building page: the Modelling tool card shows the record; Edit opens the same dialog.
  7. Remove record in the dialog cleans up test entries.

B1 · "Uses LumoCad independently"

Models without help from a colleague, or chooses LumoCad without being told? Default: a Yes/No per person typed in by you, meaning "models in LumoCad without needing help".

Maps to A8

B2 · "Uses LumoCad" and "finished a building in LumoCad" per person

Read from the building records, or typed per person like the sheet? Default: read from the buildings. A person shows as a user once their buildings have a record.

Maps to A8

B3 · Who is "the team"

Internal postprocessing only, or also preprocessing, Packing Sports, freelancers? Default: the internal postprocessing team as in LumoPlan's team view. Packing Sports and freelance measurers are not listed.

Maps to A8

B4 · "Building finished in LumoCad"

Only when delivered, or already when the LumoCad modelling is done? Default: delivered and modelled in LumoCad end to end. Started in LumoCad, finished in Revit is a switch, not a finish.

Maps to A6

B5 · Shares: of which buildings?

All buildings in production, or the ones entered? Default: of the buildings with a record, like the sheet. A separate Not tracked card counts in-progress or completed buildings without a record.

Maps to A6

B6 · How far back

Do buildings delivered before LumoCad was used need a record, or does tracking start at a date? Default: no start date; every delivered building without a record counts in Not tracked.

Maps to A6

B7 · Planned buildings

Which buildings are "Planned"? Default: a postprocessing schedule exists but nobody is booked or has logged hours. Cancelled buildings are left out.

Maps to A5, A6

B8 · Steps when switched to Revit

Which steps should the list offer? Default: Preprocessing, 3D modelling, 3D QC, 2D plans, 2D QC, Client revisions. A switch can be saved without a step.

Maps to A7

B9 · Reason for switching

The sheet has free text; LumoPlan has a list plus a note. Keep the list? Default: yes, so reasons can be counted: geometry too complex, LumoCad feature missing, LumoCad bug or too slow, deadline pressure, customer needs Revit/IFC, modeller not trained, other (note required).

Maps to A7

B10 · Planned for Revit, done in Revit

Should LumoPlan still ask why? Default: no. Nothing went off plan, so nothing more is asked; a note is optional.
